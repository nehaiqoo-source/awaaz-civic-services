# Round 2 demo video: script (under 3 minutes)

Rules: YouTube, public or unlisted, at most 3:00. Vimeo and Google Drive links are rejected. Show the product working with a real voice.

Record on a laptop with Chrome (voice needs Chrome or Edge) at https://www.awaaz.help, or on the Android app. Use QuickTime (File, New Screen Recording, with the microphone on) so your voice and the app's spoken replies are both recorded. Speak Hindi into the mic; the captions below are what you say.

Aim for 2:40 so you have room.

| Time | Screen | What you do and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Home page www.awaaz.help | Voice-over: "Many people in India can explain their problem out loud but cannot fill a government form. Awaaz lets them do it by speaking." |
| 0:15 to 0:50 | Saathi chat (/saathi), Hindi selected, tick "Read answers aloud" | Tap the mic and say: "Meri pension teen mahine se nahi aayi." Saathi asks "Which kind of pension?" aloud. Tap or say the old age / widow option. The service card appears and is read aloud. Point at "Keep ready" and "Open official site". |
| 0:50 to 1:50 | Demo form (/demo-form) | Tap the Awaaz button. Answer each spoken question by voice: your name, mobile number, date of birth, state and the reason. Show it filling each field and reading it back. Show that an Aadhaar or OTP field is skipped with "type this yourself". Show the final read-back, then press Submit yourself and say "Awaaz never presses Submit for you." |
| 1:50 to 2:25 | Complaint page (/grievance) | Tap "Tap and speak" and say: "Hamari gali mein do hafte se paani nahi aa raha." Optionally add a photo. Show the draft, submit it, and show the tracking ID being read aloud. |
| 2:25 to 2:45 | Android app home (emulator or phone) or the browser extension on another site | "The same voice flow works in the Android app and as a Chrome extension on any website." |
| 2:45 to 3:00 | GitHub repo page | "Open source, runs with docker compose up, no keys needed. Awaaz: public services for people who can't fill a form." |

Before uploading:
- Check the video is under 3:00.
- On YouTube choose Unlisted (or Public). Do not choose Private.
- Paste the YouTube link into the submission form.

If something fails while recording (the mic is blocked or speech is not recognised), stop and record that part again. Do not fake the voice part with typed text: the rules ask to show the product working.
